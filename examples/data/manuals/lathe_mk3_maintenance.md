# Safety precautions

WARNING: Disconnect main power and lock out the disconnect switch before opening the electrical cabinet. Residual voltage remains in the drive inverters for up to 5 minutes after power-off.

CAUTION: The spindle cartridge weighs 38 kg. Use the lifting jig (part no. TG-0921) and two people when removing it.

# Weekly maintenance

1. Wipe the ways and apply way oil to all four lubrication points.
2. Check the spindle bearing temperature at the front housing; it must be below 55 °C after 30 minutes of continuous operation.
3. Inspect the chip conveyor belt for cracks and remove packed chips from the corners.
4. Clean the coolant tank strainer and verify coolant concentration with a refractometer; target range is 8-10 %.
5. Run the spindle warm-up program (O0001) for 10 minutes before cutting operations.

# Troubleshooting

## Vibration during cutting

Vibration above 2 mm/s RMS usually indicates a worn spindle bearing or an unbalanced workpiece. Measure the spindle vibration at the sensor port on the front housing. Replace the spindle cartridge (part no. SP-4412) when vibration exceeds 4.5 mm/s RMS at 3000 rpm.

WARNING: Never attempt spindle cartridge replacement while coolant is flowing through the jacket. Drain the jacket first.
